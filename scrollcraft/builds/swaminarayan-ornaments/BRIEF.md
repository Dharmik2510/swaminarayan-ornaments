## 2. The Scroll Journey (Section by Section)
1. **Act I — The Sovereign Vault (Dimensional Layered Hero):** The visitor lands inside a five-plane spatial composition. A 92K emerald-and-kundan haar floats above an obsidian plinth framed by temple latticework and foreground gold bezels. Scrolling separates the depth planes and transitions the headline from the brand promise to live trade credentials.
2. **Act II — The Assay & Purity Standard (Flow + Live Metallurgy Comparator):** Real trade figures tick up once on entry (25+ years of craft, 92K & 84K purity standards, 18+ categories, 500+ retail partners) paired with an interactive 92K vs 84K metallurgical assay comparator.
3. **Act III — The Goldsmith's Crucible (Engineered Peak — Pinned Anatomy & Blueprint Wipe):** The stage pins while an SVG technical blueprint draws itself (`stroke-dashoffset` driven by `--sc-p`) around a signature ornament, wiping via `data-sc-reveal` between raw architectural linework and finished 22K gold as four craft stages (Ghaat, Chilai, Jadtar, Hallmark) crossfade. Visitors can switch specimens and inspect with an interactive loupe.
4. **Act IV — The Exhibition Rail (Horizontal Pan Lineup):** Vertical scroll translates into lateral travel across a wide exhibition rail of hero pieces with 3D pointer tilt (`data-sc-tilt="6"`) and staggered `--sc-p` arrival.
5. **Act V — The 18-Category Trade Vault & Curated Pieces (Flow + Spotlight + AI Concierge):** Full searchable category matrix and filterable featured catalog with purity toggles (`All`, `92K`, `84K`) and instant modal inspection.
6. **Act VI — Trade Dossier & Allocation Builder (Interactive Agency):** Retail buyers configure their wholesale inquiry (target carat purity, category focus, and appointment/catalog preference) which persists directly into the closing CTA.
7. **Act VII — The Sovereign Close (Pinned Finale):** A spotlight-lit closing stage with a greet-and-hold cue (`data-sc-cue="0 1 0 0"`) and magnetic WhatsApp & Catalog dispatch actions carrying the buyer's configured trade brief.
